# QProver MicroBench provenance

These six paired Solidity families are original, local educational fixtures
written for QProver under this repository's Apache-2.0 license. No public-chain
state, RPC endpoint, fork, incident source, or historical proof of concept was
copied into these fixtures.

The `a` and `b` identifiers are neutral fixture variants. Expected outcomes and
regression sequences are scorer-only data in `labels.json`; production search
inputs use only the manifests.
